# Where every number comes from

## enrollment.csv  (IIE Open Doors, academic years)
- `total_students` = all international students at US colleges, including those on OPT.
- `new_students` = students enrolling at a US school for the first time.

| Years | Source |
|---|---|
| 2018-19 to 2022-23 | Open Doors 2023 Fast Facts — https://www.jmu.edu/global/_files/2023-open-doors-fast-facts.pdf |
| 2023-24 | Open Doors 2024 press release — https://opendoorsdata.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/OD24_Open-Doors-2024-Press-Release.pdf and IIE news release — https://www.iie.org/news/us-hosts-more-than-1-1-million-intl-students-at-higher-education-institutions-all-time-high/ |
| 2024-25 total | NAFSA Fall 2025 Snapshot overview — https://www.nafsa.org/sites/default/files/media/document/Fall%202025%20International%20Student%20Enrollment%20Snapshot%20Economic%20Impact%20Overview%20FINAL.pdf |
| 2024-25 new, 2025-26 | Open Doors 2026 (released Nov 16, 2026), summarized at https://day1cpt.org/news/open-doors-2026-international-enrollment-drop |

**Fall 2026 estimate (used only as a dashed line in Chart 1):** IIE's Fall 2026 Snapshot survey of 809 schools
reported a 15% drop in new students. I applied -15% to the 2025-26 number. This is a rough estimate, not an official count.

## work_training.csv  (ICE "SEVIS by the Numbers", calendar years)
- `opt_working` = students on OPT with work authorization who reported working for an employer.
- `cpt` = students authorized for Curricular Practical Training.
- `stem_opt` = students authorized for the STEM OPT extension.

| Years | Source |
|---|---|
| 2021 | 2021 report — https://ice.gov/doclib/sevis/btn/22_0406_hsi_sevp-cy21-sevis-btn.pdf |
| 2022 | 2022 report — https://ice.gov/doclib/sevis/btn/22_0406_hsi_sevp-cy22-sevis-btn.pdf |
| 2023 | 2023 report — https://ice.gov/doclib/sevis/btn/24_0510_hsi_sevp-cy23-sevis-btn.pdf |
| 2024 | 2024 report — https://www.ice.gov/doclib/sevis/btn/25_0605_2024-sevis-btn.pdf and ICE news release — https://www.ice.gov/news/releases/ice-releases-2024-sevp-annual-report |
